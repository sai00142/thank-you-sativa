WHAT I WANTED YOU TO KNOW

Dear Sativa,

When I think about my time with SPARK, I keep returning to how much I learned simply by watching you work. You were our direct line of contact, but that phrase does not begin to capture what you were to the project—or to me. You were thoughtful, steady, responsive, and extraordinarily careful, both with the work and with the people doing it.

I admire you not only as a supervisor, but as a researcher. The care you bring to every detail never feels mechanical; it reflects how seriously you take the purpose of the work and the people it is meant to serve. You showed me that rigor can be generous, that clarity and kindness can coexist, and that good leadership is often visible in the space it creates for other people to do their best work.

I learned so much through the SPARK project itself, but I learned just as much from the way you approached it. Your example has shaped how I want to conduct research, collaborate with others, and someday support the people whose work I may be fortunate enough to supervise.

Thank you for your guidance, your patience, your trust, and the many lessons you taught without ever needing to announce them. Working with you has been a genuine privilege, and I will carry what I learned from you into every stage of the researcher—and supervisor—I hope to become.

With immense gratitude,Saira
